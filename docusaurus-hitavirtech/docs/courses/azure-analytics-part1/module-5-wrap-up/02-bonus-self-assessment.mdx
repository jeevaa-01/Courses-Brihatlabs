<LearningObjectives>

- Run any real project through the five diagnostic questions
- Score each V from 1 (healthy) to 5 (painful) to locate your bottleneck
- Map your bottleneck V to the Azure services worth studying first

</LearningObjectives>

```
  +==============================================================+
  |        DIAGNOSE  YOUR  OWN  PROJECT  WITH  THE  5  Vs        |
  +==============================================================+
```

Think of a data project you work on (or want to build). Run it through these five questions. The V that feels **most stressful** is your bottleneck — that's where to focus first.

![ADLS Gen2](/img/courses/azure-icons/adls-gen2.svg) ![Data Factory](/img/courses/azure-icons/data-factory.svg) ![Synapse](/img/courses/azure-icons/synapse.svg) ![Event Hubs](/img/courses/azure-icons/event-hubs.svg) ![Stream Analytics](/img/courses/azure-icons/stream-analytics.svg) ![Defender](/img/courses/azure-icons/defender.svg) ![Power BI](/img/courses/azure-icons/power-bi.svg) ![ML](/img/courses/azure-icons/ml.svg)

| # | Question | Your V | Azure Services to Study |
|:-:|----------|:------:|-------------------------|
| 1 | "Do we have somewhere cheap and durable to store everything?" | 📦 Volume | 🪣 ADLS Gen2 • 🏛️ Synapse • 🔥 Databricks |
| 2 | "Do we have to handle more than one data format?" | 🧩 Variety | 🕸️ ADF • 🔍 Synapse Serverless • 🤖 AI Vision • 📝 Doc Intelligence |
| 3 | "Is the current data freshness good enough for stakeholders?" | 🌊 Velocity | 🌊 Event Hubs • 🎯 Stream Analytics • ⚡ Functions |
| 4 | "Do stakeholders trust the numbers we publish?" | 🛡️ Veracity | 🧪 ADF Data Flows • 🛡️ Purview • 🕵️ Defender |
| 5 | "Is anyone actually acting on our outputs?" | 💎 Value | 📊 Power BI • 🤖 Azure ML • 🎯 Personalizer |

Score each V from 1 (healthy) to 5 (painful). The highest score is where a senior engineer should lead the next sprint.

:::tip[Pro Tip]
"Stacking solutions for Velocity when Veracity is the real problem is the most
common and expensive Azure mistake. Diagnose honestly before you build."
:::

<KeyTakeaways>

- The five diagnostic questions turn the 5 Vs into a practical project audit
- Score each V 1-5; the highest score is your bottleneck and your next sprint's focus
- Each bottleneck V comes with a short list of Azure services to study first
- Misdiagnosing the bottleneck (e.g. buying streaming when trust is the problem) is the most expensive mistake

</KeyTakeaways>
