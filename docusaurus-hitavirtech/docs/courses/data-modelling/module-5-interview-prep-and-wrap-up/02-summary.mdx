### What You Mastered

![What You Mastered](/img/courses/diagrams/what-you-mastered.svg)

:::tip[HitaVir Tech says]
"Data modelling is the most underrated skill in Data Engineering. Tools change every year. Cloud platforms evolve every quarter. But a well-designed data model lasts for decades. Master this, and you will always be in demand."
:::

<KeyTakeaways>

- You progressed from conceptual/logical/physical models through star schemas, SCDs, and enterprise architectures
- The golden rule holds everywhere: normalize where data is written, denormalize where data is read
- Architecture patterns (Kimball, Inmon, Data Vault, medallion) are complementary tools, not competitors
- A well-designed model outlives every tool and platform it runs on

</KeyTakeaways>
