<KeyTakeaways>

- Official AWS docs follow one URL shape: docs.aws.amazon.com/(service)
- The Well-Architected Analytics Lens and Modern Data Architecture whitepapers are the canonical follow-ups
- Free hands-on labs exist for Glue Studio, Lake Formation, Redshift Serverless, Kinesis, and QuickSight
- Kleppmann, Kimball, and Reis are the three books that deepen everything in this course

</KeyTakeaways>
